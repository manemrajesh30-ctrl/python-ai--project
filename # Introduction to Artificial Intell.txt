# Introduction to Artificial Intelligence

## What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a field of computer science that focuses on creating systems that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

Examples:
- Chatbots
- Voice assistants
- Recommendation systems
- Self-driving systems
- Image recognition

## What is Machine Learning?

Machine Learning (ML) is a subset of AI where computers learn patterns from data and use those patterns to make predictions or decisions.

Examples:
- Spam detection
- House price prediction
- Fraud detection
- Movie recommendations

## What is Deep Learning?

Deep Learning is a subset of Machine Learning that uses artificial neural networks with multiple layers to learn complex patterns from large amounts of data.

Examples:
- Face recognition
- Speech recognition
- Image classification
- Large language models

## AI vs ML vs Deep Learning

AI
→ Broad field of making machines intelligent.

ML
→ A subset of AI that learns from data.

Deep Learning
→ A subset of ML based on multi-layer neural networks.

## Conclusion

AI, Machine Learning, and Deep Learning are important technologies used to solve real-world problems and automate intelligent tasks.
